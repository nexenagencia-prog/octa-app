# OCTA Black/Silver Productivity Design

Approved scope: preserve the current Home/hero structure while unifying product surfaces around black, graphite, white and liquid-silver. Agenda meeting cards and pricing cards become black/silver. Calculator becomes a premium black/silver floating/page tool. The Home exposes the current subscription as a discreet `Plano Pro` badge.

The whiteboard keeps drawing/shapes/undo/redo/share and gains a non-clipped full-height page canvas, text objects, bold toggle, font sizes through 72px, stroke width, selection-friendly smart object erasing and a denser productivity toolbar.

Instant Meeting reuses the same permanent DashboardSidebar used by the Home instead of maintaining a divergent sidebar. Its stage, controls and participant surfaces use black/silver. Adding participants opens a thumbnail picker with search and direct WhatsApp invite action while preserving the existing room controls.

Skills is renamed visually to `Skills`; its black/silver dashboard remains intact and its bars, ring, trend line and radar animate from an empty/loading state on page mount. Blue/cyan accents are removed from the new/updated product surfaces in favor of graphite and liquid-silver, while semantic success/destructive states remain legible.

Validation: a new contract covers these requirements; existing relevant contracts are updated only where prior expectations conflict with the explicitly approved newer design. Build/typecheck and deployment status must be verified before completion.
